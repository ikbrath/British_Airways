# British_Airways

✨ I’m thrilled to share that I’ve recently completed the British Airways Data Science Job Simulation through Forage! This virtual experience allowed me to step into the role of a data analyst at British Airways, where I explored real-world datasets, performed exploratory data analysis (EDA), and built predictive models to help optimize customer booking behavior.

Through this program, I gained valuable hands-on experience in data cleaning, feature engineering, and model evaluation, as well as developed my skills in Python, data visualization, and business insight presentation. The project also enhanced my problem-solving and critical-thinking abilities by simulating challenges that data professionals face in the aviation industry.

I’m grateful to Forage and British Airways for this incredible learning opportunity — and I look forward to applying these skills to future data-driven projects! 🚀
